NLO Bleach Tray

Makes the bleaching-tray model from a scan, with the gel reservoirs, the Trimlign base and the trim line, and puts it into OrthoFactory as an order. Nothing to pay per case.

Everyday use takes 2 minutes

1

Export the patient's scan from the scanner, with the patient's name in the file name (the scanner does this on its own: Jane_Doe_20261015_09-20-31_upper.ply). Choose PLY when the scanner offers it — it carries the colour, and the app uses the colour to find the gum line (pink against white) and to throw out gum it would otherwise take for a tooth. STL works too, from shape alone.

2

Drop the file on the Bleach Tray app page (home page → Bleach Tray). The app asks what to make — whitening tray and/or retainer (TT) — and whether the opposing arch is coming: drop that one too, answer for it, then Start. Nothing runs before Start. A whole day's scans at once, or files dragged into S:\clinical\3D printing\BleachTray, run with the default (both) without questions. Several people dropping at the same time is fine — cases run one after another.

3

A few minutes later the case shows "check & send" in the app with four models: whitening tray (WT) and thermoformed retainer (TT), upper and lower. Look at the 3D result — trim line, reservoirs, position on the base. Fix a tooth with the brush (the ball on the model is the brush; hold Shift and drag, then Apply fixes & re-run), Cut away a tongue, cheek or flap the scanner caught, paint No reservoir over a recession the app should keep gel away from or Block out under a pontic or implant crown so the retainer seats, or nudge the base if something is off. Chose only the retainer and need the whitening tray too? Also make … in the Check & send box adds it from the scan already there.

4

Click Send to Trimlign. Only then does the patient appear in OrthoFactory (the Trimlign software) → Production, like any Titan case (whitening trays as Template pieces, retainers as Aligner pieces). Print on the Form 4B, thermoform, trim on the Trimlign. Discard throws it away. Save as lesson keeps a brush-corrected case as a lesson for the app without ordering anything (Send does that too); it lights up once a fix has been applied.

Nothing reaches OrthoFactory without that click. Files dragged into the folder move into BleachTray\done when processed, or into BleachTray\failed with a note explaining what happened. The small black window called "NLO Bleach Tray" must be open on the PC that runs the app (Dr. A's PC for now; it starts by itself at sign-in; if it's gone, double-click start_app.bat in the app folder).

If a case needs a look optional

Open the app from any office computer and drop a scan anywhere on the page: it asks what to make, processes, and shows the result for a check — nothing reaches OrthoFactory until someone clicks Send. See the result in 3D, paint over a tooth the computer missed (the ball on the model is the brush; hold Shift and drag), re-run; move the base plate under the arch with Position on the base (drag the plate to slide it, the blue ring to turn it — the view flips to the underside so the locating holes can be kept clear of the arch; Apply rebuilds, Reset goes back to automatic). It runs on Dr. A's PC for now, so that PC has to be on. Clicking Bleach Tray on the NLO apps home page opens it directly.

Open the Bleach Tray app

One-time setup on the PC that runs the app about 10 minutes, one person, once

1

Get the app folder onto the PC: download nlo-bleachtray.zip from Dr. Akhavan (or from the code page → green Code button → Download ZIP), right-click it → Extract All, and put the nlo-bleachtray folder somewhere permanent, for example C:\NLO\nlo-bleachtray.

2

Open that folder and double-click install.bat. A black window walks you through everything:

  • If the PC doesn't have Python 3.13 yet (the free program the app runs on), it downloads the right installer for you. Run it and click Install Now. Then double-click install.bat again. (A newer Python already on the PC is fine; both can stay.)
  • It installs the app's components (a few minutes), finds the Titan folder on its own, learns the Trimlign base from one of your existing Titan exports, and creates the BleachTray drop folder next to the Titan folder.
  • When it asks "Start the watcher automatically every time this PC signs in?", type y. From then on the app starts by itself, watching the drop folder and serving the browser app to the office.
3

Double-click start_app.bat once to start it now. Done — go to Everyday use above.

Good to know

  • Default is whitening tray + retainer for each arch: the whitening tray has 0.5 mm reservoirs on the front teeth to the second premolars and a trim line at the gum line; the retainer's line runs 1.25 mm onto the gum. "Make" in the app switches to one of them only.
  • Orders show up in OrthoFactory with the whitening trays as Template pieces (TU0A / TL0A) and the retainers as Aligner pieces (AU1A / AL1A), in one order per patient.
  • Past cases: every case stays on file. Type a name or a date under Past cases in the sidebar, click it, and it comes back as it was: print it again, Send to Trimlign again for a lost print or a second set, or fix and re-run. Model files are kept 30 days; older cases keep their recipe (scan + settings + fixes, ~15 MB an arch) and re-make themselves in a minute when opened.
  • Exposed roots get no gel: the reservoir stops 1 mm short of the gum line, and where a tooth shows root (recession, a crown margin) the app finds it on its own and keeps the reservoir a further millimetre above - the result line says on which teeth. For retainers, undercuts deeper than 0.75 mm - under a pontic, round an implant crown, an open embrasure - are filled like block-out resin (shown in teal) so the sheet seats and comes off; smaller natural ones stay for retention.
  • The model is based the way Titan does it: levelled to the gum line (low, even walls), a 3 mm rim beyond the trim line, ending 3 mm behind the last molar with the tissue there lowered out of the bur's way, and nothing loose left lying around. A brush fix or a cut is kept for every later re-run of that case.
  • Braces on: a scan taken at the eval appointment can go straight in - tick Braces on - take them off first on the scan (it is never done on its own: on a plain scan the detector can mistake stained enamel for a bracket). On a coloured (PLY) scan the app then finds the brackets, wire and ties by colour and shape, cuts them out, closes each hole with smooth new enamel that follows the crown, looks once more for anything left at the bracket bases, and then makes the retainer from the clean teeth. The result line says how much came off; the brush view shows the rebuilt enamel in grey, and a bit it missed is painted with the Take off brush and re-run. Best results: wire and ties off before the eval scan, brackets on. Clear brackets and molar bands are not handled yet.
  • Mouthguard (braces on): tick Mouthguard (MG) on the scan. The model keeps the brackets, wire and tubes under a smooth shield with 1 mm of room (the sheet forms over a smooth channel instead of locking into the brackets), the trim line runs as deep into the vestibule as the scan goes - buccal and labial - with a rounded notch at the labial frenum (and at a buccal frenum when the scan shows one), 6 mm onto the palate, and round the last tooth at the back. Scan with the cheeks and lip pulled back as far as you can: that is how deep the guard will go. In the brush view the shielded parts are orange; Take off adds a part the app missed to the shield. Goes to OrthoFactory as a stage-2 piece (MG).
  • Missing teeth: a missing premolar or molar no longer ends the tray at the gap — the trim line is carried across the ridge to the teeth behind, and the molar behind the gap is still treated as a molar. (Building the span up to tooth width is on the list.)
  • Smooth distal ends: the trim line finishes round each last tooth as a gentle curve — rounded corners, no dip or flap behind the molar, and never past the tooth onto the pad or tuberosity. A last tooth the scan did not capture whole gets the tray cut 2 mm short of the scan edge, with the same curve over the tooth.
  • Settings (the gear in the New case card): every number has a picture of what it means — reservoir region and depth, the trim lines, rim, floor depth, marks, bulk defaults. Remembered in the browser; Back to defaults resets.
  • It learns by itself: nothing to install or download for this. Every case you send is saved under training\<Last, First> - <date>\ in the app folder (scan + the tooth labels you sent, marked when you used the brush). A Titan export dropped on the page (Name - 0 - Maxilla.stl) is filed into the same patient folder, and one with a shell per tooth counts as a lesson too. Between 1 and 5 am, when nothing is running and there are new corrections, the app retrains the model, checks that the new one really does better on the corrected cases without getting worse on the base set, and only then starts using it. The sidebar shows the model's state; Retrain now under Settings runs it on the spot.
  • The app runs on Dr. A's PC and is reachable on the office network only. From home, open it through Jump Desktop (or any remote desktop into an office PC); it has no address on the internet.
  • Every model carries the patient's initials + W or R raised on the cheek side of a first molar — it forms into the tray itself, like an aligner maker's mark — and, when the arch leaves room, the name and WHITENING / RETAINER engraved on the base plate inside the arch (Settings to switch off).
  • Processing takes about 30–40 seconds per arch.
  • Something looks wrong on a case? Send Dr. Akhavan the patient name and the files from BleachTray\done\… (or failed\…).
  • Scanner integration: under Settings, save the scanner's export folder once. The app then lists the recent scans (patient, date, upper/lower) and Make tray processes them in one click — no dragging or re-exporting.
  • Updating: on the PC that runs the app, press Win + R, paste the line below, press Enter. It fetches the newest version from GitHub, keeps the settings, and restarts the app (ends with "UPDATE DONE").
    powershell -NoExit -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "[Net.ServicePointManager]::SecurityProtocol='Tls12'; iex (iwr 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amooloo/nlo-apps/main/nlo-bleachtray/update.ps1' -UseBasicParsing).Content" (On a PC that allows .bat files, double-clicking update.bat in the app folder does the same.)
