# NLO A/R — source

Encrypted accounts-receivable worklist that replaces the two Google Sheets made from Edge's A/R Aging report on 20 Sep 2026 (`NLO-AR-aging-worklist` and `NLO-credit-balance-worklist`). The page people open is `../nlo-ar.html` (built from here). It uses the same Firebase project (`nlo-cases`) and the same logins as NLO Cases and NLO Leads, but **its own key**: only Dr. A and the people he turns A/R on for can open anything in it.

## How it works
- **In:** Edge → Reporting → Financial → *Accounts Receivable Aging* (Today; tick *Exclude Zero Dollar Balances*; leave *Exclude Insurance Contracts* unticked; Subgroup: None) → View → Export ▾ → Excel. The file is dropped on Reports (or pasted from Excel). It's read in the browser (`edge.js`: .xls BIFF8, .xlsx, CSV/tab text, Excel's clipboard HTML), checked against Edge's own total line, and only the accounts past due or in credit are kept, plus the whole book's totals. Nothing leaves the computer until it's sealed.
- **The lists** (`ar.js`, pure functions — the 20 Sep worksheets' rules, reproduced exactly on the real report): 91+ ranked by the 91+ amount with the 70% line; 31–90 and 0–30; insurance where *nothing* has been paid (Dr. A's rule: past due that is an exact multiple of the $11.11 instalment = N untouched months), triaged by days (≤60 monitor, ≤120 chase now, ≤365 investigate, older probably never filed); credit balances oldest first (3+ years, then inactive, then over a year; *Start Scheduled* prepayments parked). The numbers are owner Settings (`meta/settings.ar`).
- **Working an account:** one tap logs a call / text / letter / carrier call / claim filed or denied / refund requested; each sets the next follow-up on an office day (Mon–Thu, handbook holidays skipped); undo; promise-to-pay with a date; assign; ask Dr. A (his Today shows it until he answers); the refund checklist from the FC instructions; resolve (paid, written off, refunded, transferred…) and reopen. An account record exists only once someone does something with it. When a new report no longer shows a worked account, it's listed under *Cleared in Edge*; one resolved earlier but on a new report again is marked *On the list again*.
- **The collections ladder** (AISA handbook §14 "Escalation timeline — 7-letter sequence", with §16 and §45): every patient account past due (not insurance, not inactive) is at the step its days past due call for — Edge's *Days* (the most days past due of any unpaid charge, on the report's date) plus the days since the report. A text on day 0; letters #1–#7 at 14, 30, 45, 60, 90, 100 and 120 days; calls at 45 and 75; each with its Weave text (*FC- Delinquent #1–#9*). Dr. A signs #4, #5, #7 and #8 ("Ask Dr. A to sign" puts it on his Today, or he marks it signed himself); #5, #7 and #8 go certified and keep their tracking number. Earlier steps that were never recorded are passed, not stacked up. *Past due → Steps due* (the first tab) lists them by step, and one step can be marked for many accounts at once (e.g. a week's Letter #2s sent together from Edge). Maintenance Hold (§45 — the FC's call from day 60) is recorded on the account, with the reminders to put the yellow box on / take it off in Edge and tell the clinical team. A payment plan (Alternative Arrangement) or a promise to pay pauses the account's ladder; *Arrangement broken* brings Letter #8 (off the sequence), then 30 days, then Debond/Finish. After Letter #7: 30 days, then the write-off. An account resolved and past due again starts a new round. The handbook names no day-75 text and no day for *FC- Delinquent #8*: the app uses #7 on day 75 and #8 on day 104 (two weeks after Dr. A's day-90 letter). It all lives in the account's sealed record (log entries), so no rules change was needed.
- **Goals** (AISA handbook §19, Practice KPIs — patient delinquency and insurance delinquency, each *no more than 4%*): two gauges at the top of Today and Summary, from the newest full report (Subgroup None, insurance included). Counts of accounts, not dollars. Patient: accounts 30+ days past due (31 days on, the Month-End 30+ column) ÷ patient accounts on the report that aren't Inactive in Edge — the handbook names no aging bucket, and Dr. A set 30+ on 6 Oct 2026 (counting 0–30 days, under 4% is too hard to hold); Settings can count from day 1 (anything past due) instead. Insurance: insurance accounts more than the "monitor up to" days past due (60) ÷ insurance accounts with a balance — the handbook counts claims on Edge's Insurance Aging report; this is the closest the A/R Aging gives. Each gauge says how many accounts stand between today and the goal. Summary also draws both rates report by report with the goals as dashed lines (from each report's sealed totals, `sum.kp`). The goals and the counting rule are owner Settings (`goalPt`, `goalIns`, `kpiFrom` in `meta/settings.ar` — no rules change).
- **The weekly report** (handbook §19: "Weekly: FC runs AR Aging"; no day named): due every Tuesday by default — the day of the FC's weekly checklist in CADANCe (owner Settings: Monday–Thursday; a holiday moves it to the next office day). A week's report is in once the newest report (one with past-due accounts) is dated that week — Monday on, even before its due day. From the due day until then, Today, Past due, Insurance and Credits show a banner (amber on the day, red once it's late) and Reports shows *Due* / *Late* in the menu; Reports also says when the next one is due. In-app only, by Dr. A's choice (6 Oct 2026). Replaced the old "say the report is old after N days" setting (`staleDays`, now ignored).
- **Focus mode** (`focusEntries` in `ar.js`): one ranked list of what to do first today, across the ladder, follow-ups, insurance, credits and Dr. A's OKs — one line per account, its most urgent reason first (the rest under "Also"), with a rough time for each. *Urgent*: OrthoBanc's failed-payment report on its days; a promise to pay whose day passed; a letter waiting 3+ office days for Dr. A's signature; a step 7+ days late on an account already moving up the ladder (an account nobody had started on isn't "late" — its step is just due); insurance within 30 days of its carrier's timely-filing limit, or with nothing paid past the chase window; a patient account reaching day 90 or 120 this week; a balance of $500+ new this week; a follow-up 3+ office days late. Today shows a one-line summary ("Do these first: 6 urgent · 33 for today · about 3 h"); Focus mode shows only the list and what's been done today (remembered per computer).
- **Insurance carriers**: Edge's A/R Aging names the policyholder on insurance rows, not the carrier, so each insurance account's carrier is set once (from the account, or Insurance → Carriers; other insurance accounts of the same policyholder get it too). Carriers keep a phone line, portal, payer ID, fax, timely-filing limit and notes. Carriers tab: a card per carrier (past due, nothing paid, oldest and average days, paid since the last report, last call), "Called about all N" (logs the carrier call with its note on each account), and the accounts without a carrier. A Carrier column and filter on the Insurance lists; the carrier (Call, Portal, payer ID, timely-filing warning) on the account's panel. Carriers, tags and OrthoBanc checks live in one sealed record kept like an account's (`arItems`, id = keyed hash of `BOOK_KEY`, `book: 1`) — no rules change; it's re-sealed with everything else on a key change.
- **Trends** (Summary): week over week from the newest two reports (`flowOf`; also kept with each new report's sealed totals as `sum.fl`): got current, newly past due, slid to an older bucket (into 91+), got better, past due that went away, the change in past due; the pace to each goal (`paceTo`, a straight line through the last up to 8 weekly rates); who did what in the last 7 days. **The weekly brief**: one button sends AISA (`aisa-worker…/ask`, in the page's CSP) the totals on the page — never a name or an account — and shows its plain-English read with three things to do, citing the handbook.
- **December credit audit** (handbook §15): Credits → December audit, December 1 – March 31: every credit (prepayments aside) with whether it's been reviewed since December 1 (anything logged or resolved), refunds still to cut, and the days left; a line on Today during the audit; unreviewed credits are "today" in Focus. Outside the season the tab says when it starts.
- **OrthoBanc's failed-payment report** (handbook §14, day 0): OrthoBanc's "Failed Report" email (to accounting@) only says the report is ready, so the app reminds on the 5th, 12th, 19th and 26th (the office day each lands on) — on Today and first in Focus — with the link to OrthoBanc's reports page, until someone marks that report checked (how many failed payments, a note). A report skipped is pointed out with the next one.
- **OrthoBanc's report file, imported** (optional, 6 Oct 2026 — the reminder and *Checked it* work as before): OrthoBanc's *Failed Transaction Report* (FailedTransactions.xls) can be imported from the reminder (*Import its file*), the *Checked it* box, or dropped/pasted on Reports (`readReportFile` tells it from Edge's report). `edge.js` reads it: each failed draft is two lines (status · OB Reference # · Edge's account # · patient · responsible · amount; *Return Reason* · reason · draft date · balance), in two sections — *Action On Your Part Is Needed* (on HOLD: two drafts or more failed, OrthoBanc stopped drafting) and *No Action Required* (OrthoBanc contacts them). A preview, then it's saved sealed in its own record per report day (`arItems`, key `OB_KEY` + the day — not the carrier book, so a tick doesn't copy the book into the history; no rules change), and it counts as that report checked. The list: on HOLD first; each family's phone number and the account when it's on the A/R report (`obMatch`: the patient's name, and the responsible party's has to agree — no match rather than a guess, since a wrong phone number is worse than none; never an insurance account); Edge's account # for the rest; *Failed before* from earlier imports (same OB reference or account #); a tick per family as texted, or *Mark all texted*. One line in Focus for the whole list (urgent while someone on HOLD is left), a banner on Today until everyone is ticked (latest report day only), and a read-only note on the account's panel. Importing the same report again replaces the list and keeps the ticks. **It never changes an account** — no note, follow-up or ladder step — and never touches the FC's own *Checked it* entry.
- **Links into A/R and the FC's checklist:** `nlo-ar.html#today`, `#focus` (Today in Focus mode, and Focus mode stays on), `#pd`, `#ins`, `#cr`, `#sum` or `#reports` opens that screen once, after signing in (anything else → Today; the `#` is taken off the address, so a reload or Lock goes back to Today). The FC's lists in CADANCe (`cadence.html`, the `nlo-arlink-addon` script) point here: any checklist item whose title or a subtask mentions "NLO A/R" gets an *NLO A/R ›* link in its title, to the screen the text names ("NLO A/R → Credits" → `#cr`; no screen named → `#today`); tapping it doesn't tick the item.
- **Tour** (as in NLO Cases): a 5-minute hands-on tour in practice mode — `nlo-ar.html?demo&tour=staff` (signed in as a staff member, "Practice User") or `&tour=owner` (Dr. A, who also signs a letter and sees Settings). Made-up accounts, nothing saved, its browser storage kept apart from the real app's (`demoSandbox`), no idle lock; Lock pauses it and signing back in carries on. Each step rings one part of the screen; most wait for the trainee (tap a step on Today, open the practice account, record its letter, log a voicemail, …) and move on by themselves; Back, Skip and End; wandering off goes back to the step that gets there again; on a phone the card is a sheet at the bottom. Offered once per person per computer on Today, and any time from My account.
- **Summary:** the goals, totals, aging, the collections ladder today, patient vs insurance, 91+ concentration, nothing-paid insurance, credits by age/source/status, the trend across reports, and the Month-End "Accounts" numbers (with Copy).

## Security
- Reports (gzip + AES-256-GCM, bound to the report id) and account records (AES-GCM, bound to the record id) are sealed in the browser. Firestore holds only the sealed copies plus dates, counts, open/done and who saved (staff id) — never a name, a phone number or an amount. Account record ids are a keyed hash (HMAC from the first A/R key) of the account, not the name.
- The **A/R key ring** (`meta/arKeys` = current version) is separate from the office key. Each person's copy is sealed to their own key pair (`arAccess/<uid>`, ECDH P-256 + HKDF), and one copy to the office recovery key (`meta/arEscrow`) so Dr. A's recovery code can always restore it. Turning someone off deletes their copy (the rules refuse them at once) and makes a new key; every report and record is sealed again under it (a few grants per batch, within the rules' lookup limits). When a login is removed or reissued in NLO Cases, the next time Dr. A opens A/R the old login's copy is deleted and a new key is made; for a reissued login he turns A/R on again himself (A/R can't tell a reissued login from someone new given the same username), and the app tells him so.
- Every save of an account record comes with an append-only history entry holding the copy it replaced (`arLog/<record>_<rev>`), so nothing can be overwritten without a trace; the owner can restore any version.
- Rules: `../nlo-cases-src/firestore.rules` (one file for the whole project — the A/R block is at the end). `OWNER_EMAIL=<Dr. A's sign-in email> python3 build.py` writes `dist/firestore.rules`; the page also carries the whole file for its "publish the rules" card. **Dr. A publishes the rules himself** (Firebase console → Firestore → Rules).
- The page: strict CSP (only this inline script, by hash; the Firebase SDK pinned to 10.12.2 with integrity hashes; sign-in frames only from the project's own domain); it refuses to run inside another page's frame; no data in localStorage except the last username; locks after the office's idle time, and locking reloads the page so nothing from A/R stays in the tab's memory; keys only in memory (a reload means signing in again). Everything read back from the database is type-checked before it's shown. Hostile files can't hang the tab (size and span limits, single-pass parsing). Downloaded lists warn first and can't carry spreadsheet formulas.
- Account records are never deleted (not even by Dr. A), so a record's history can't be restarted or skipped; history entries must be sealed with the current key.
- Known, shared with NLO Cases and NLO Leads: the pages live on the same web address as the office's other tools (amooloo.github.io), so a script running in one of those pages could reach an open A/R tab. Moving the three apps to their own Firebase Hosting address (as NLO Vault is) would close that.

## Files
- `src/` — `core.js` (helpers, office days, crypto), `edge.js` (reading Edge's export, and OrthoBanc's failed-payment report), `ar.js` (the rules, summary, what changed, working an account, the collections ladder), `backend.js` (Firebase), `demo.js` (`?demo`, made-up accounts), `ui.js` (sign-in, the A/R key, live updates, shell), `views.js` (Today, Past due, Insurance, Credits, Summary, Reports), `panel.js` (the account panel), `admin.js` (Settings, My account), `tour.js` (the new-user tour and practice mode), `style.css`, `template.html`.
- `python3 build.py` → `dist/nlo-ar.html`. `fbconfig.js` is the live Firebase web config (public by design; security is the rules plus encryption).

## Tests (made-up data only)
- `python3 test/make_fixtures.py` writes made-up Edge exports in the real layout to `test/fixtures/` (.xls with merged cells and a split string table, .xlsx, tab text, clipboard HTML); `python3 test/make_ob_fixture.py` a made-up OrthoBanc Failed Transaction Report (.xls).
- `node test/unit.test.js` — the reader on every format, the rules, summary, Month-End, what changed, working an account, the collections ladder, the goals, the weekly report's due day (Tuesday unless set), focus ranking, carriers, week over week, the pace to the goals, the credit audit, the OrthoBanc reminder, OrthoBanc's report file (reading it, matching names to accounts, the import record and ticks, earlier failures), office days, sealing.
- `node test/tour.js` — walks the tour in practice mode as staff and as Dr. A: the ring on target and the card in view, wandering off, Back, Skip, End, Lock and picking up again, no idle lock, storage kept apart from the real app, the plain demo unchanged, phones (static server on :8766 serving `dist/`).
- `node test/demo_smoke.js` — clicks through `?demo` on a desktop and a phone (including importing OrthoBanc's report from the reminder and from Reports, the list, ticks, Focus, the account's note, and that no account changed), and opens it from links (`#reports`, `#cr`, `#focus`, `#pd`, anything else) (static server on :8766 serving `dist/`).
- `NO_PROXY=localhost,127.0.0.1 npx firebase emulators:exec --only firestore,auth --project demo-nlo-cases "node test/rules.test.mjs && node test/e2e.js"` — the rules (every A/R request, plus what someone without A/R or a removed person would try), and end to end with NLO Cases: set-up, giving and taking A/R, importing, two people at once, Dr. A's OK, a letter he signs on the collections ladder, OrthoBanc's report pasted, ticked and seen live (sealed, re-sealed on a key change, no account touched), the tour offer, key changes, a reissued login, the owner's password reset, removal, framing (static server on :8767 serving `nlo-ar.html` next to `nlo-cases.html` and `logo-white.png`).
- The browser tests serve Google's exact Firebase SDK files (the page checks their integrity); `test/helpers.js` fetches them once into `test/vendor/` (git-ignored).
- After changing the shared rules, also run NLO Cases' and NLO Leads' rules tests (`test/rules.test.mjs` in each, after their `build.py`).
