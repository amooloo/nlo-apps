# NLO Cases — source

Encrypted case tracker that replaces Asana + Tally for patient cases. The page people open is `../nlo-cases.html` (built from here).

- `src/` — `core.js` (config, case types/stages, crypto helpers, Asana parsing, and the search box read as an appointment day — `apptQuery` / `apptQueryText`: today, tomorrow, Thursday (or any start of it from 3 letters), next Thursday, Thursday after next, this / next / last week, a week from today, the week of Nov 9, the next 10 days, in the next week, November, next May, 11/5, Nov 5th, the 5th, Monday to Thursday, before / after / by Friday, Thursday and Friday, Nov 5 and 6, Thursday and Friday next week, with the words around them and any times left out ("deliveries next week", "what's due Thursday", "Thursday at 2 p.m.") and a day at the start or end of other words, which narrow it down ("oliv next week"; "Oliv and Angel next week" = either) — words that can't (show me, this, sometime, morning) are dropped, a word it can't take in beside a day (after, until, except…) means it isn't read as a day at all, beside other words a weekday's non-standard start (Frida) or a lone short form beside a day that isn't a weekday (Sun tomorrow, Sun 11/5) is taken for a name, while a weekday that is that very day is only a reminder (tomorrow Thu);
