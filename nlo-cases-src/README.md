# NLO Cases — source

Encrypted case tracker that replaces Asana + Tally for patient cases. The page people open is `../nlo-cases.html` (built from here).

- `src/` — `core.js` (config, case types/stages, crypto helpers, Asana parsing), `backend.js` (Firebase: sign-in, key ring, encrypted cases, enforced version history, team, key change; the patient index — pn / pc on each case, keyed hashes of the patient's name and chart #, so an app fetches the earlier cases of the patients it shows instead of every completed case (the owner's app indexes the office once the newer rules are live, then catches up every 6 hours on cases saved without it); deletes marked in their history entry; the backup file: every case as stored, sealed, with its photo, the team list, the settings and the recovery box — opened with the office key or the recovery code it was made with), `demo.js` (`?demo`, made-up data), `ipr.js` (read-only link to the IPR Tracker's database), `ui.js` (incl. the chart note: the scan visit's and the delivery visit's, each with what the patient was told for that kind of case — `NOTE_KINDS`, suggested wording the owner rewords in Team & security → Chart note, saved as the `noteInstr` map in `meta/settings`; and the open-case list's Notes column: the newest comment or Notes, with who wrote it — a Notes saved in the app records `notesBy` / `notesAt` / `notesH`, an older one is looked up once per computer in the case's sealed history and remembered by case id as `nloCases.noteAuth`; the assigned person on the list and on board cards is a button that opens the team to reassign the case without opening it; what the case is — "Refinement 2 Next Level Aligners" — at the top of its panel, `caseTitle`; each Stage step marked with when it was reached and by whom, from the case's history, `stageMarks`; the IPR Tracker's latest visit kept on each case as `iprSnap` — saved whenever a case is read while the IPR link is on, and for every open aligner case with a chart # at once by Sync all patients (also run by itself once the link is on, at most every 15 minutes per computer) — so the chart stays after the link ends and shows on every computer), `logos.js` (company logos for the case-type tiles, generated), `caseform.js` (tap-first New case form; a refinement's number, `refN`, starting on the next after the patient's refinements on file with that company; retainers and whitening trays can be a remake w/ or w/o the model on file, `remake`), `admin.js` (Team & security, incl. Backups — download a sealed backup, Today's monthly reminder, restore the cases a backup has that NLO Cases doesn't (in this office, or a new one with the old recovery code) — incl. adding people from Staff Hub's office roster `nlo/cadence/roster` read through the IPR link's Google sign-in, and the one-time card that hands out newer security rules), `mail.js` (lab-email updates: opens the sealed emails the Gmail script forwards, reads the uLab / Partners / Oliv / Angel formats, matches each patient to a case — lab case # first, then name — and moves cases forward; the Today card for updates that need a case; the Team & security card that hands out the script), `photos.js` (patient photos: cropped and shrunk to a 160 px JPEG in the browser, sealed with the office key in `photos/{caseId}`, changed only together with a save of the case; shown next to names, loaded when on screen, "Hide photos" blurs them on that computer), `rx.js` (Specialty Appliances' Rx forms — the shared engine plus the Herbst Rx: the choices tapped in New case / Edit / the case's Rx section, the appliance drawn on Specialty's own arch outlines plus pen/line/arrow, the estimate from Specialty's price list MKT-41, and Specialty's form filled in as a PDF made in the browser — an incremental update appended to the blank form, no library; Dr. A's account #, license # and signature line, price changes and the usual choices per form in Team & security → Lab Rx, kept in `meta/settings`; each form is a "kind" in `RXK`, saved on the case in its own field: `rx` Herbst, `rxRet` Retainer, `rxMet` Metal, `rxFun` Functional), `rxret.js` (Specialty's Retainer Rx, MKT-7: Hawley and spring designs, clasps / pontics / finger springs / spurs / resets tapped on the teeth, accessories, acrylic options, fixed lingual and clear retainers, upper and lower as on the paper; filled in from the case's Hawley arches and acrylic color; Dr. A's clasps — Adams to start, delta and arrowhead written out with their teeth — his photos with Preferred / Recommended, spurs facing distal or mesial, the pontic shade required), `rxmetal.js` (Specialty's Metal Rx, MKT-6: RPE, MSE and other metal appliances), `rxfun.js` (Specialty's Functional Rx, MKT-12: a lower Schwarz starts as Dr. A designs it — midline screw, delta clasps on the 6s, ball clasps at the D–E contacts, lingual horseshoe, no occlusal acrylic — Specialty's standard colors only), `rxdraw.js` (generated by `tools/rxdraw.py`: the clasp drawings and the lower Schwarz picture on the info cards), `rxdata.js` (generated by `tools/rxforms.py`: the blank forms, their circles, lines, grids and tooth outlines), `style.css`, `template.html`.
- `rx/specialty-herbst-source.pdf`, `rx/specialty-retainer-source.pdf`, `rx/specialty-metal-source.pdf`, `rx/specialty-functional-source.pdf` — Specialty's Herbst Appliance Rx (MKT-005 Rev 3-25), Retainer Rx (MKT-7 Rev 2-25), Metal Rx (MKT-6 Rev 10-25) and Functional Rx (MKT-12 10-23), with their fill-in fields; `python3 tools/rxforms.py` (needs pikepdf, pdfplumber, Pillow, reportlab and pdftoppm) takes the fields out, keeps where each circle/line/grid/tooth is, adds the fonts the filled-in text uses and writes `src/rxdata.js` plus `../nlo-cases-rx-herbst.png`, `../nlo-cases-rx-retainer.png`, `../nlo-cases-rx-metal.png` and `../nlo-cases-rx-functional.png` (grey pictures of the blank forms the app shows under what's filled in; they sit next to the page and the build copies them into `dist/`). A newer revision of a form: `python3 tools/rxforms.py herbst|retainer|metal|functional path/to/new.pdf`.
- `pics/<key>-source.png` — Amir's pictures (case types, scanners, instructions, Herbsts, Hawleys, clasps) and the acrylic color chart; `python3 tools/pics.py` writes `src/pics.js` — small ones as data: URLs, the Hawley and clasp photos and the 34 acrylic swatches (cut from the chart) as WebP files in `../nlo-cases-pics/`, next to the page (the build copies them into `dist/`).
- `logos/<type>-source.png` — the company logos Amir pasted (Oliv, Angel, uLab, Invisalign, InSmile); `python3 tools/logos.py` trims them, makes the white transparent and writes `src/logos.js` (data: URLs, allowed by the page's CSP). Add a logo by saving it as `logos/<type>-source.png` (type = the tile key, e.g. `oliv`) and re-running it.
- `mail/` — the Google Apps Script that runs in each office Gmail account (every 10 minutes): `script.js` (the script), `seal.js` (BigInt-free P-256 ECDH + HKDF-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM, the same sealing as the app's `Crypto.sealTo`, since Apps Script has no WebCrypto), and `nlo-cases-mail.gs`, built by `node tools/build-mail.js` (bundles `seal.js` with esbuild and checks it against WebCrypto known answers). The script forwards whole lab emails sealed to the office inbox key, using its own robot login that can only add sealed items to `inbox/` and note its check-ins in `mailbeat/`; all parsing is in the app, so a new lab format only needs an app update. The app hands out a copy with the robot login filled in (Team & security → Email updates).
- `firestore.rules` — security rules; `__OWNER_EMAIL__` is filled in by the build. The app checks which set is live by reading a doc only the newer rules allow (`photos/_rules_check`: photos and email updates; `meta/rules_20261004`: the patient index and marked deletes) and hands the owner the new set on Team & security until it's published. `test/rules-prev.rules` and `test/rules-1004.rules` are the older sets the e2e test switches to.
- Backups: Google keeps a daily backup for 98 days and a 7-day point-in-time recovery (both turned on 4 Oct 2026 in the Firebase console, Firestore → Disaster Recovery; a restore goes into a new database). The owner also downloads a sealed backup file from Team & security about monthly (Today reminds after 30 days).
- `python3 build.py` → `dist/nlo-cases.html` + `dist/firestore.rules`. `fbconfig.js` holds the live Firebase web config (project `nlo-cases`; public by design — security is the rules plus encryption). For the rules that go live, build with `OWNER_EMAIL=<Dr. A's sign-in email>` and publish `dist/firestore.rules` in the Firebase console (Firestore → Rules). Without `fbconfig.js` the page is demo-only.
- Tests (need `npm i` with `package.json` — firebase-tools, @firebase/rules-unit-testing, firebase@12, firebase10, playwright, and for the email script elliptic, aes-js, hash.js, esbuild — and a static server on :8765 serving `dist/`):
  `NO_PROXY=localhost,127.0.0.1 npx firebase emulators:exec --only firestore,auth,database --project demo-nlo-cases "node test/rules.test.mjs && node test/e2e.js"`, plus `node test/ipr_parity.js`, `node test/demo_smoke.js` and `node test/mail_script.js` (the email script against the app's own crypto and a fake Firestore). `test/gas.js` mocks Apps Script; the e2e test runs the handed-out script against the emulators with made-up patients.
