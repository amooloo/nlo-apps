Position on the base

While this box is open the base has handles: drag the plate to slide it, drag the thin blue ring to turn it - the arch stays where it is. The mark on the model (the raised initials + W/R that form into the tray) shows as a small card on the model: drag it onto any tooth or onto the side of the model, turn it and size it with the Mark buttons, choose what it says, raised or cut in; Auto puts it back on a first molar. Fold the box and they go away. The buttons are fine steps (mm and degrees); the base moves live under the arch. Underneath looks up at the underside: the locating holes in the base plate must stay clear of the arch. Apply moves this arch's models on the base (a few seconds - a height change rebuilds them, ~1 min) and replaces the Trimlign order; the other arch stays as it is. Reset goes back to the automatic placement.

Text on the model

The text is part of the model: raised it forms into the tray and reads on it (the way aligner makers mark theirs), cut in it stays on the model only. Place: click a start point and an end point on the model and the letters are laid along the surface between them, sized to fit; or drag the yellow card. Turn and size with the buttons. Auto puts it back on a first molar. Apply makes it (a few seconds - the model itself is kept).
