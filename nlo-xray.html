NLO X-Ray Reader

Traces lateral cephs, measures them, and shows the skeletal class, vertical pattern and CVM stage. It learns from every tracing you confirm. Everything stays on the office PC.

Everyday use a few minutes a ceph

1

Export the ceph from the imaging software - DICOM is best (it carries the patient, the date and the pixel size); a JPG / PNG / TIFF works too. Drop it on the X-Ray Reader page (home page → X-Ray Reader) and check the name, x-ray date, birth date and sex.

2

Click Sella, Nasion and Menton. Every other point appears roughly in place, and the app walks you through them one by one: click where the point belongs, or press Enter when it is right. (Once the app has learned - after about 15 confirmed tracings - it places all the points itself.)

3

The measurements, skeletal class, vertical pattern and CVM stage update as you go. Confirm tracing files it as a lesson; Print gives a one-page report.

Open the X-Ray Reader

Install or update one line, on the PC that runs it

On the PC that should run the app (it has to be on, also in the evening when it learns): Win + R, paste this line, Enter. It installs Python if needed, the app and its components, starts it at sign-in, and opens it. The same line updates it later - the x-rays, tracings and settings are kept.

powershell -NoExit -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -Command "[Net.ServicePointManager]::SecurityProtocol='Tls12'; iex (iwr 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amooloo/nlo-apps/main/nlo-xray/setup.ps1' -UseBasicParsing).Content"

It ends with the PC's name. If that is not , use "Use a different PC" below once in each browser, or change DEFAULT_PC in nlo-xray.html.

Private by design

  • The x-rays, tracings and the trained network stay in the app folder on that PC (C:\NLO\nlo-xray). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • The app makes no internet connections, and its page cannot load or send anything outside the office network.
  • It learns only from tracings confirmed in this office - no outside data, no pre-trained models.
  • An optional office passcode (Settings → Passcode) keeps it to staff computers if guests share the Wi-Fi.
