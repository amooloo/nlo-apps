X-Ray Reader

Drop a lateral ceph to trace it, measure it, and see the skeletal class, vertical pattern and CVM stage. Every tracing you confirm teaches the app to place the points itself.

Drop a ceph here DICOM from the imaging software, or a JPG / PNG / TIFF export. Several at once is fine. Or click to choose.
1

Check the details

Name, x-ray date, birth date and sex come from a DICOM file; for an exported picture, type them.

2

Trace

Click Sella, Nasion and Menton. The other points appear roughly in place: click where each one belongs, or press Enter when it is right.

3

Confirm

Measurements, class and CVM update as you go. Confirming makes the tracing a lesson for the app.

Private by designThe x-rays, tracings and the trained model stay in the app folder on this PC. The app makes no internet connections, and the page cannot load or send anything outside the office network.

Import from iOrtho

Drop the files hereor click to choose

iOrtho's points are found on its traced image, laid onto the original x-ray, and kept only where they reproduce iOrtho's own numbers (SNA, SNB, U1-SN, FMA, IMPA, the lips to the E-line and more). iOrtho does not trace the vertebrae: they are skipped on an imported x-ray (untick Skip vertebrae to trace them).
